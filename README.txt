Gailson Media LLC Organization Website
====================================

Files:
- index.html
- projects.html
- contact.html
- styles.css

Deployment:
Upload these files to any static host such as Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages,
Netlify, or a standard web host. Point your Gailson Media domain to that host.

Before using the site for Google Play organization verification:
1. Confirm the legal business name exactly matches your Google Payments / D-U-N-S record.
2. Replace the contact email if Gailson Media LLC has a dedicated organization email.
3. Consider adding your publicly listed organization phone number and business address
   to Contact Us if those are already public and match your D-U-N-S record.
4. Use a domain you control and verify that domain through Google Search Console
   if Play Console asks you to verify website ownership.
